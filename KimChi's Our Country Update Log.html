Major Philippine Updates

  • Impeachment Trial Update:

    Prosecutors have opted to drop 15 witnesses and instead plan to call and grill Vice President Sara Duterte directly on the stand, with the impeachment court also revisiting the "2/3" conviction voting rule.

  • Cabinet Appointment:

    Former boxer and senator Manny Pacquiao has joined the Marcos administration as the new anti-poverty czar.

  • Labor and Economy:

    July unemployment surged to 6 percent, marking the highest rate in the post-pandemic era, while the Philippine peso hit a new record low.

  • Visayas Power Crisis:

    Power brownouts loom as the Visayas grid was placed under a red alert status.

  • Weather and Infrastructure:

    Orange rainfall warnings and class suspensions were declared in Metro Manila and surrounding areas due to persistent habagat rains, which have contributed to billions in agricultural damage alongside recent cyclones.
