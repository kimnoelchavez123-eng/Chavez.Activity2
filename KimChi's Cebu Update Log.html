Top Headlines in Cebu Today

  • Air Quality and Haze:

    Metro Cebu's air quality has improved slightly to the "unhealthy for sensitive groups" category, easing from previous "very unhealthy" and "acutely unhealthy" levels caused by Indonesian wildfire haze.

  • Passing of Resil Mojares:

    The local government and community are mourning the death of National Artist and renowned Cebuano scholar Resil Mojares at age 83, with flags flown at half-mast.

  • Ombudsman Suspends CPA Chief:

    The Office of the Ombudsman placed Cebu Port Authority General Manager Francisco C. Comendador III under a six-month preventive suspension over grave misconduct allegations.

  • Crime and Safety:

    Police reported a fatal shooting incident involving an ex-convict near the Inayawan dumpsite in Cebu City earlier today.

  • El Niño Preperations:

    The Cebu City government met with stakeholders to prepare coordinated responses for potential water shortages and crop damage as a moderate to strong El Niño persists.
