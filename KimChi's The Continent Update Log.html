Major Continental Updates

  • The New World Map

    The UN approved a non-binding resolution on September 4, 2026, endorsing the Equal Earth map projection. Led by Togo and supported by African nations, the vote was 164 in favor with only the United States opposed. This map shows Africa, Australia, and Latin America at their true relative sizes, making them appear larger compared to the traditional Mercator projection which enlarges North America and Europe.

  • Future Supercontinents

    Researchers using NASA climate models recently highlighted how Earth's continents might eventually drift together over millions of years to form new supercontinents like Pangaea Ultima, Ora, or Amazia, drastically altering global weather patterns and sea levels.
